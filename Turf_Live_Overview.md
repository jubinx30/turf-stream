# Turf Live

## Overview

Broadcasting amateur sports and managing grassroots athletic venues poses significant technical and commercial hurdles on conventional video platforms. Mainstream streaming platforms require expensive proprietary cameras, charge recurring broadcast fees, or lock live matches behind cumbersome account registration walls, leaving local turf operators, players, and families underserved. Spectators frequently struggle to view live matches without friction, while venue managers miss out on lucrative post-match replay monetization and simple camera setup options. Additionally, evaluating multi-turf availability from afar and navigating complex video ingestion pipelines create persistent operational friction. Turf Live solves these challenges by delivering a dedicated, camera-agnostic live streaming and monetization platform tailored exclusively to sports turfs, athletic arenas, and local tournament facilities.

## Key Features of the App

* **Interactive Geospatial Turf Mapping**: Browse arenas and turf grounds visually with dynamic live status indicators, interactive map markers, and GPS geolocation. Switching across venues provides an eagle’s eye view of match action and facilities.
* **Frictionless Public Live Feed**: Watch high-definition, low-latency HLS match broadcasts instantly directly in the browser with zero mandatory account registration or upfront barriers.
* **Automated 2-Hour Replay Paywall**: Stream and download match recordings for free within a 2-hour post-match grace period, seamlessly converting older sessions to paid access via Single Match Passes ($1.99) or All-Access Subscriptions ($9.99/mo).
* **Universal Hardware & Camera Agnosticism**: Connect any camera hardware without custom software—turn everyday Android phones, PoE IP cameras (Hikvision/Dahua), Raspberry Pi 3 setups, or action cams (GoPro RTMP) into automated broadcast hubs.
* **Turf Directory & Multi-Sport Filtering**: Filter athletic grounds by sport type, amenities (floodlights, changing rooms, turf condition), operational hours, and active live stream availability.
* **Integrated Turf Slot Booking**: Reserve match slots, schedule team games, and verify court availability with instant confirmation modals and automated fixture scheduling.
* **Direct Venue Inquiries & Support**: Contact facility managers directly through quick communication channels for private tournament hosting, coaching bookings, and field rental guidance.
* **Secure Media Downloads & Replays**: Download verified, high-bitrate match videos with token-gated security headers and chunked HTTP range streaming to prevent unauthorized distribution.
